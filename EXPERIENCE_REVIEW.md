# Try the experimental experience

1. Open **Make It Yours** from the pull-tab menu, or **Enter the Sweet Lab** from the hero.
2. Pick rose, yellow, or orange, enter your own label, and switch between satin and gloss. Drag the 3D preview and try **Spin It**. Reload to check that the design remains saved on this device.
3. Open **Stick & Play**. Drag across two photos with Tape selected. Choose Move and drag one of the joined photos: both move together. Choose Peel to remove the strip; Undo restores it. Add Strip and photo arrow keys provide keyboard controls.
4. Scroll through the finale, or press **Let It Roll** in the studio. Your named roll joins the bouncing footer products. Click the custom footer roll to return to the studio.
5. Drag the fixed **Pull Me** tab downward to stretch it and open the menu. Hover a lifestyle photo to lift its edge.

The previous design is saved as `before-experimental-experience` / `de15b2c`. Saying “go back” is enough to request the rollback.
